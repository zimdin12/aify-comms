## Every tag runs the same flow

1. Plan, one page at most, sent to comms-senior-dev **before** building.
2. Build with a test for each fix, watched failing without the fix.
3. All suites green, then self-review of the diff.
4. comms-senior-dev reviews the diff, including any change made after its verdict.
5. Tag, deploy, `aify-comms doctor` and `aify-env doctor` green, then the operator is told.
