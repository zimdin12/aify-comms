## The scan now walks every NAME, not just call sites

Slice 2's map measured helper *calls* and declared the surface was three small functions. It was
wrong: `_ENVIRONMENT_HEARTBEAT_STATUSES`, `SPAWN_ORPHAN_GRACE_SECONDS` and
`_TERMINAL_END_STATUSES_ORDERED` are **constants**, invisible to a call scan, and the move failed
three times with 372 red tests before they were all found.

The scan for this slice walks every `ast.Name` load in each function body and intersects it with
every module-level binding — functions *and* constants. It caught `LIVE_SESSION_STATUSES` on the
first run, which the old method would have missed in exactly the same way.

## Verdict: split, like slice 1 — 3a now, 3b deferred

**3a — the three session functions, 419 lines.** Dependency surface is one constant
(`LIVE_SESSION_STATUSES`, which moves with them) and `_load_settings`, which gets the established
scalar seam. Clean.

**3b — `_agent_liveness`, deferred.** Only 45 lines, but it pulls the whole liveness-helper family:
`_agent_has_live_terminal`, `_has_live_channel_sidecar`, `_resident_bridge_is_fresh`. `api_v2.py`
carries a standing TODO above `_agent_liveness` saying those helpers have "many callers [which] make
ripping them out a separate, risky migration" — the router's own note, written before this release.
Moving 45 lines by dragging that family across is not slice 3; it is the liveness-consolidation the
TODO describes, and it deserves its own slice with its own dependency map.

That makes two deferrals now (1b: status core, 3b: liveness family), both for the same reason — a
small function anchored to a large unmoved cluster. Neither should be allowed to become permanent:
they are the two places where the extraction will have to decide whether to consolidate or to leave
a seam.

## `_load_settings` — third use of the same seam

`_reconcile_dead_session_status` and `_reconcile_duplicate_resident_sessions` both read settings.
Same treatment as slices 1a and 2: identify the keys, derive the scalars in the caller from the
pass settings, pass them as REQUIRED parameters. Read the actual keys before writing the signature —
slice 2's orphan reaper turned out to need exactly one (`environment_offline_seconds`), and assuming
otherwise would have hoisted more than necessary.
